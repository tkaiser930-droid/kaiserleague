2026-11-14 trade alerts and Triple Crown Supercross scoring:

2026-11-14 trade response notification lifecycle update:
- Accepted/rejected trade responses remain as one-time alerts under the sender team's name in Team Management until that team opens the Trade Center.
- Opening the Trade Center marks all accepted/rejected outgoing trade notifications for that team as viewed (responseViewedAt), and the alerts disappear from Team Management.
- Pending outgoing and incoming trade alerts are unchanged.
- Existing trade records without a viewed timestamp are treated as unread, so prior unanswered-to-view outcome alerts are not lost during this update.

Official results importer (November 15, 2026): Added Commissioner > Scoring > Import Official Race Results. The Node server can preview official HTTPS race-result HTML tables from allowlisted series domains, or parse official CSV uploads. Matched results are loaded into the existing scoring table for review; scores are not saved until the commissioner submits the scoring form. CSV requires Position and Rider/Driver columns. Best Lap can preselect the generic Fastest Lap bonus when supplied. See RESULTS-IMPORT-SETUP.txt for source support and limitations.

F1 importer refinements (November 15, 2026)
- Retains Formula 1 result rows with official position NC; the preview displays NC and assigns consecutive internal scoring slots after classified finishers in the exact order shown by the official page.
- For Grand Prix scoring, also fetches the event's official /qualifying page and identifies the P1 driver for the existing Fastest Qualifier bonus.
- For Sprint scoring, fetches the event's official /sprint-qualifying page and identifies the P1 driver for the existing Fastest Qualifier bonus.
- Automatically preselects the +3 Fastest Qualifier checkbox when the top qualifier matches a driver row loaded into the race/sprint scoring table.
- Grand Prix fastest-lap fetching remains unchanged; Sprint scoring does not use the Grand Prix fastest-laps page.

Official result athlete-matching correction (November 15, 2026)
- A unique exact rider/driver name match now takes priority over a conflicting official race number for every supported series (SMX 250/450, Triple Crown Series, MXGP, MXON, F1 and MotoGP).
- When the name matches but the official number differs from the league athlete's number, the row remains matched and can be loaded; the preview clearly flags the official and league numbers for commissioner review.
- Race number remains a fallback when the official name is missing or does not match an eligible league athlete. Number is only used to disambiguate duplicate exact names and cannot redirect a clear name match to a different athlete.
- Added regression tests covering name-first matching, number-only fallback, duplicate-name conflicts, all supported series and already-scored protection.

2026-11-16 Commissioner season archive support:
- Added a commissioner-only season selector directly below the Commissioner Dashboard heading, with Current and historical seasons 2026 through 2016.
- Each historical year lazily creates an independent archive containing its own athlete catalogue, fantasy team roster copies, score records, trade proposals/notification state, draft picks, draft settings, and event schedule.
- All Commissioner Tools read and write the selected season: athlete add/edit/delete and team assignment, scoring/results and scoring history, draft order/status/settings, schedule management, and Yearly Reset.
- Historical athlete/race-team/manufacturer edits update only the selected archived athlete and roster records; they do not change the current live athlete catalogue, race-team assignments, or fantasy rosters.
- Historical season data is stored under kaiserFantasySeasonArchives, persisted by the Node server and included in Render/GitHub JSON backups and Data & Backup export/restore.
- Current public pages remain connected to the Current season only; archived rosters and schedules are only accessed through the Commissioner dashboard.
- Athlete profiles now show the previous season's average points per round alongside current-season average. Average points use total archived score points divided by unique event rounds; multiple motos/segments in one round count as one round.
- Selecting Current uses the 2026 archive as the previous-season source; selecting a historical year uses the preceding year (2016 has no prior archive in the configured range).
