Score Race Results

Choose a series and division, then enter the finishing order. All athletes are selectable, including free agents. Base points follow the SMX 22-place model. Moto 1, Moto 2, and sprint-winner bonuses are worth 5 points; fastest qualifier, heat winner, qualifying race winner, and sprint fastest qualifier are worth 2 points; fastest lap is worth 1 point.

Select riders by finish position. Bonus checkboxes are independent for each rider, so a bonus can be assigned to multiple riders or left unused.

Previously Scored Events

Choose Edit Scoring to load an event’s results into the 1–22 scoring table. Saving replaces the selected event and division’s old results instead of adding duplicates.
